## title
[proud] Torque. [pause] Knock-out leverage on Nvidia, settled in U.S.D.G., on Robinhood Chain.

## problem
[thoughtful] Robinhood Chain holds almost seven hundred million dollars of Paxos U.S.D.G. [pause] But only about one point two million dollars is lent against stock tokens... and almost all of it is already borrowed. [intrigued] People want leverage on stocks. The dollars to fund it are not there.

## product
[optimistic] Torque is leverage that brings its own liquidity. [pause] Pick Nvidia, choose two to five times, and pay in U.S.D.G. [slowly] The most you can ever lose is what you put in. [pause] There are no margin calls.

## how
[content] When you open, the vault lends the rest, and the whole position is bought as real Nvidia in the Uniswap pool, within one percent of Chainlink's price. [pause] If Chainlink prints at your knock-out level, anyone can close it. The vault is repaid first... and the rest is yours.

## solvency
[thoughtful] The hard part is keeping the vault solvent. [pause] Every position is fully hedged with real Nvidia, and exposure is capped in the contract. [pause] Before every action, two safety checks run: the Chainlink price must be fresh, and the pool's thirty-minute average must agree with it. When the feed freezes for the weekend... Torque stops.

## evidence
[proud] The solvency invariants were written before the product code. [pause] Then an adversarial pass tried to drain the vault. It found one real problem, a dead price feed that could have locked the money, and it was fixed before deployment. [pause] The tests catch every planted bug that changes the contract's behaviour... twenty-four of them.

## usdg
[content] U.S.D.G. is not bolted on. [pause] It is the margin, the liquidity, the loan... and the payout.

## field
[thoughtful] Against the field: Gauntlet's leverage is an off-chain game, priced by the operator's own server. Torque settles on chain, against the real Nvidia price. [pause] Undertow's vault lends a stablecoin it mints itself. Torque's counterparty is a real U.S.D.G. vault... and every loan is secured by real Nvidia.

## status
[content] Torque is built and tested against Robinhood Chain mainnet state, but it is not deployed there. [pause] The evidence is a full rehearsal on a fork of mainnet: the real U.S.D.G., Nvidia, pool and Chainlink feed, the same deploy script, and real transactions. Testnet has no real stock feeds or stock pools, so it would prove nothing. [pause] Next: mainnet, shorts, and more stocks.
