How the vault stays solvent

Fully hedged

Every position's whole notional is bought as real NVDA in the NVDA/USDG pool and held by the contract. The vault is a secured lender, never short NVDA.

Hard caps, in the contract

Vault ≤ $20. Open interest ≤ $30. Lending ≤ 80% of the vault. Leverage 2–5×. Every fill within 1% of Chainlink.

Two safety checks

A fresh Chainlink print (12h) and agreement with the pool's 30-minute average (1.5%). When the feed freezes for the weekend and NVDA keeps trading, TORQUE stops opening, knocking out and taking LP flows.

Weekend gap, tested

If Monday opens below a position's financing level, the knock-out fires on the first fresh price. The trader loses the margin, never more. LPs absorb exactly the shortfall, which was already in NAV.

Knock-outs can't be blocked

Residuals are credited for the trader to claim, so a frozen USDG address can never hold up a knock-out. Fast sell-offs aren't held up by a lagging average either.

No admin key

No owner, no pause, no upgrade. 9 solvency invariants, 32 unit tests, 23 attacks and 6 mainnet-fork tests. Every planted bug that changes behaviour, 24 of them, is caught.

How a position works

  1. Open. You post margin. The vault lends the rest. The whole size is bought as NVDA on-chain.
  2. Hold. Debt accrues at 10% APR. Your knock-out barrier sits 5% above the price where the position is worth zero.
  3. Close. Sell back into the pool any time the vault is repaid in full. You keep the rest.
  4. Knock-out. If Chainlink prints at or below your barrier, anyone can close you out. The vault is repaid; what's left is yours.
