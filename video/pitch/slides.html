How a position works

01OpenYou post margin. The vault lends the rest. The whole size is bought as real NVDA in the Uniswap pool, within 1% of Chainlink.
02HoldDebt accrues at 10% APR. The knock-out sits 5% above the price where the position is worth zero.
03CloseSell back into the pool. The vault is repaid in full; you keep the rest.
04Knock-outChainlink prints at the barrier: anyone can close it. Vault first, the rest is yours.
